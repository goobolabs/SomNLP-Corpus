## Flagged

- **Somali TinyStories** has no license on its dataset card (recorded as `Other`), and repeats
  20,900 of its 42,000 stories verbatim upstream; merge keeps one copy of each.
- **FinePDFs** tokenizes 10.9% above the v2 holdout mean, in line with Wikipedia and mC4. v2 was not
  retrained; see [tokenizer/PAPER.md](../../tokenizer/PAPER.md) §6.4.
- Near dedup keeps the longest member of each cluster, so longer FineWeb-2 copies displaced some
  HPLT, mC4, MADLAD, and XL-Sum documents.
